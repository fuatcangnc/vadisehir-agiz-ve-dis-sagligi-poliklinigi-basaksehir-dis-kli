description: "A short checklist that saves time and worry."
publishedAt: "2026-10-15"
category: "Guides"
image: "/images/questions.webp"
---

Before treatment starts, be clear about what needs doing, what it will cost, how long it will take, who will do the work and what aftercare involves.

Asking these five questions at the start makes every later decision easier.
